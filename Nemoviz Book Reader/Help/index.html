Help — coming soon

The manual for Nemoviz Book Reader is being written. This page is here so that F1 always leads somewhere: the failure people notice is pressing a key and getting silence, not getting a page that is still short.

Until it is written, most of the app explains itself where you are standing. Every group of settings carries a small question mark, and F1 opens the same explanation from wherever the focus already is — in Settings, in a book's Properties, and in the pronunciation dictionary.

This page opens in your own browser, deliberately: you get heading navigation, find-on-page, your own fonts and colours, and your screen reader set up the way you like it. None of that would be true of a window the app drew for itself.
